Quill Bbfy Manga Gets TV Anime Adaptation for January 2027

Studio Kumoji animates Rin Takamiya's cozy bakery manga; teaser and key visual revealed

by Quill Xpsb · Oct 4, 2026 8:30 AM JST · 32 comments

The official website for Rin Takamiya's Quill Bbfy manga announced on Saturday that the series is getting a television anime adaptation that will premiere in January 2027. The announcement came with a 40-second teaser trailer and the key visual above.

Studio Kumoji is animating the series, with Hiroki Sawada directing and Naoko Ishimori in charge of series composition. Ayumi Kase is adapting the original character designs for animation.

The announced cast includes:

  • Quill Jcbb — Morning Ledger Hmbatxp
  • Quill Pbym — Kenta Mori
  • Quill Kqxv — Emiko Tsuji

Takamiya launched the manga in Seiran-sha's Monthly Comic Hoshizora magazine in 2021. The ninth compiled book volume shipped in August, and the series has more than 1.8 million copies in circulation. The story follows Kotone, who inherits her grandmother's bakery in a seaside town and discovers that the bread she bakes at night grants small wishes to whoever eats it.
